Personal Google Assistant

A private Hermes assistant that helps its owner use Google Calendar and Gmail.

The assistant runs on a Hostinger-hosted instance and uses two separate Google OAuth projects: one for Calendar and one for Gmail. Access requires the owner's explicit Google consent for each integration.

What the integrations do

Google Calendar
Reads events, checks availability, and creates or updates events. Uses the calendar.events and calendar.freebusy scopes.
Gmail
Reads and sends email and manages mailbox state, such as labels and read status. Uses the gmail.modify scope.

Relevant email and calendar content may be processed by the configured AI provider and delivered through Telegram as part of the owner's assistant workflow.

An information website

This website describes the integration only. It does not connect visitors' Google accounts, provide the assistant itself, or grant access to anyone's Google data. The assistant is for its owner's personal use and is not a public service.
