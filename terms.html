Personal use

Personal Google Assistant is a private, single-user Hermes integration for its owner's Calendar and Gmail tasks. It is not offered as a public service, and this website does not offer accounts or access to the assistant.

Information only

This website explains the integrations. Visiting it does not connect a Google account or grant access to the assistant or anyone's Google data. The owner's explicit Google consent is required for each integration's access.

Use of the assistant

The owner is responsible for authorizing access and reviewing assistant outputs and actions, including emails and calendar changes. AI-generated responses may contain errors, and continued availability is not guaranteed.

Data handling is described in the privacy policy.
